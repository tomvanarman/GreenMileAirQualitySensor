# Stakeholder Questions: The Green Mile

## Fragen an Stakeholder

- What is your vision for the Green Mile?
- Who is the Climate Box intended for?
- Why do you want to measure air quality in Amsterdam?
- Why did you choose the Stadhouderskade as the location?
- Why should everyone be able to build their own Climate Box?
- How should people be able to tell whether the air quality is good or bad?
