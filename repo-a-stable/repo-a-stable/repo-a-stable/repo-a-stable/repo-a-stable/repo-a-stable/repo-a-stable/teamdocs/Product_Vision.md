## Product Vision Board — The Green Mile Project

---

### Product Name

**Green Mile Pollution Box**

---

### Vision Statement

To empower communities to take ownership of their local air quality by providing an affordable, energy-efficient, and scalable pollution monitoring network — enabling anyone to measure, understand, and take informed action on the air they breathe.

---

### Target Group

- **Civic communities:** Environmentally conscious citizens and neighborhood groups advocating for cleaner urban air.
- **Urban residents:** People living in high-traffic or industrial areas who want ongoing visibility into local pollution levels.
- **Makers and educators:** Hobbyists, students, and teachers interested in open-source hardware and hands-on environmental sensing projects.
- **Municipalities and urban planners:** Local governments seeking low-cost, distributed air quality data to support evidence-based policy decisions.
- **Researchers:** Academics and scientists conducting hyper-local, field-level air quality studies.

---

### Needs

| Need | Description |
|---|---|
| **Long-lasting operation** | Users need a device that runs for extended periods without frequent recharging or hands-on maintenance, ensuring maximum uptime during field deployment. |
| **Stable, always-on connectivity** | Pollution data is only valuable if it is reliably transmitted. SIM-based cellular internet ensures consistent data uploads even in challenging urban radio-frequency environments. |
| **Scalable area coverage** | A single sensor provides limited insight. Users need a coordinated network of devices, spaced approximately 100 meters apart, that communicate with each other to build a complete picture of neighborhood-level air quality. |
| **Accessible self-build documentation** | The project's impact grows with adoption. Clear, jargon-free build instructions allow anyone, regardless of technical background, to replicate the device independently. |
| **Proven real-world reliability** | Lab results alone are insufficient. Users and stakeholders need confidence that the device performs accurately and durably under actual urban conditions, including heat, humidity, vibration, and dust. |

---

### Product

A compact, battery-powered air pollution sensing device designed for distributed urban deployment.

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Battery** | Optimized for extended life through systematic hardware analysis, evaluation of LED removal, and hardwired circuit boards to minimize power loss. |
| **Connectivity** | Primary: SIM card-based cellular internet for reliable, uninterrupted data upload. Secondary: Wi-Fi for local dashboard access and device configuration. |
| **Network** | Mesh-capable architecture supporting at least 5 devices communicating peer-to-peer over approximately 100 meters. |
| **Sensors** | Core air pollution sensing suite, with optional motion sensor integration for proximity-triggered measurements or user interaction logging. |
| **Form Factor** | Current physical size has been validated as optimal. No design changes are planned. |
| **Documentation** | Comprehensive, step-by-step self-build guide delivered as the final project artifact. |

---

### Value

| Stakeholder | Value Delivered |
|---|---|
| **Individual users** | Affordable, hands-on access to hyper-local air quality data without dependence on expensive commercial monitoring stations. |
| **Communities** | A grassroots tool that raises awareness, fuels public conversation, and provides evidence for advocacy around urban air pollution. |
| **Makers and educators** | A fully documented, open-source project that can be built, studied, extended, and taught in classrooms or makerspaces. |
| **Researchers** | A scalable mesh of low-cost sensors producing distributed, real-world pollution datasets suitable for academic and policy research. |
| **Municipalities** | Data-driven insight into neighborhood-level air quality at a fraction of the cost of traditional monitoring infrastructure. |

---

### Business Goals

| Priority | Goal | Success Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | **Energy Efficiency** | Measurable battery life improvement achieved through reverse engineering, hardware optimization, and systematic power-consumption reduction. |
| High | **Connectivity Reliability** | Stable SIM card-based internet connectivity established. Existing Wi-Fi and dashboard connection issues fully resolved. |
| Medium | **Scalable Deployment** | A working network of at least 5 interconnected devices demonstrated with reliable communication at approximately 100-meter spacing. |
| Medium | **Real-World Validation** | Field tests completed in urban environments. Public feedback collected on air pollution awareness and device usability and acceptance. |
| Final Deliverable | **Open Documentation** | A comprehensive, self-contained build guide delivered, enabling independent replication of the Pollution Box by anyone. |
| Ongoing | **Iterative Delivery** | Disciplined sprint cadence maintained with weekly progress updates and monthly public showcases, ensuring continuous transparency and stakeholder confidence. |
